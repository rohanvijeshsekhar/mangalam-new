    BOOKING CHECKLIST

    What to Check Before Choosing a Travel Agency

    If you are comparing the best travel agency in Trivandrum for your trip, look beyond the headline price. A useful quotation should help you understand:

    1. 01

      The itinerary

      Places covered, overnight stays and travel between destinations.

    2. 02

      The accommodation

      Proposed hotels, room category and meal plan.

    3. 03

      The transport

      Flights and transfers included, with relevant conditions.

    4. 04

      The total cost

      Taxes, additional charges and optional experiences.

    5. 05

      The booking terms

      Payment dates, amendment charges and cancellation conditions.

    6. 06

      The contact arrangements

      Whom to reach before departure and during the trip.

    Bring these questions to your conversation with Mangalam. A clear understanding of your booking helps you choose with confidence.
